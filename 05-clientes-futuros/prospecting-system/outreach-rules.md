# Outreach Rules — Dxien Studio

## Main Rule

Do not spam. Contact fewer businesses with better, more personalized messages.

## Before Contacting

- Check the business website or store.
- Identify one real improvement opportunity.
- Make sure the business fits Dxien Studio services.
- Do not send the same generic message to everyone.
- Do not criticize aggressively.
- Do not promise guaranteed sales.
- Do not ask for passwords or private access.

## Message Style

Good messages should be:

- Short
- Friendly
- Specific
- Honest
- Helpful
- Non-pushy

## Avoid Messages Like

- “Your website is bad.”
- “I guarantee more sales.”
- “Pay me and I will fix everything.”
- “I am the best Shopify expert.”
- “You need my service urgently.”

## Better Approach

Mention one specific thing that could improve and offer a small helpful suggestion.

## Follow-up Rule

Only send 1 follow-up after 3–5 days.

If they do not reply, stop.

## Safety Rule

Keep conversations professional. Do not accept suspicious links, external payments or unclear projects.
