# Daily Prospecting Routine — Dxien Studio

## Time Needed

30–45 minutes per day.

## Daily Goal

Find and evaluate 3–5 potential businesses.

Contact only 1–2 if they are a strong fit.

## Step 1 — Find Businesses

Possible sources:

- Instagram
- Google Maps
- Shopify stores
- Facebook groups
- Local businesses
- Beauty/skincare brands
- Spas and salons
- Barber shops
- Aesthetic clinics

## Step 2 — Quick Review

Before contacting, check:

- Does the business have a website?
- Does the page look professional?
- Is the CTA clear?
- Is the mobile experience good?
- Are trust elements visible?
- Could Dxien Studio realistically help?

## Step 3 — Record in Tracker

Add the business to prospect-tracker.md.

## Step 4 — Personalize Message

Mention one real observation.

Do not send generic spam.

## Step 5 — Send Message

Send only if the business is a good fit.

## Step 6 — Follow Up

Follow up once after 3–5 days.

If there is no reply, stop.

## Weekly Goal

- 15–25 businesses reviewed
- 5–10 personalized messages sent
- 1–3 conversations started

## Important

Quality is more important than quantity.
