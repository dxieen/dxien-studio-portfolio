# Client Selection Criteria — Dxien Studio

## Ideal Clients

Dxien Studio should focus on small businesses and e-commerce brands that need a cleaner, more trustworthy and more professional online presence.

## Best Client Types

1. Small Shopify stores
2. Beauty brands
3. Skincare stores
4. Spas
5. Barber shops
6. Beauty salons
7. Aesthetic clinics
8. Local service businesses
9. Small e-commerce brands with weak product pages
10. Businesses with traffic but poor page presentation

## Good Signs

- The business already has a website or Shopify store.
- The page looks basic, outdated or confusing.
- The mobile layout is weak.
- The CTA is not clear.
- Product or service benefits are not easy to understand.
- There are few trust elements.
- The brand has Instagram activity.
- The business posts products or services regularly.
- The business could benefit from a landing page or page audit.

## Bad Client Fits

Avoid businesses that:

- Ask for guaranteed sales.
- Want a full complex website for very low payment.
- Want to copy another brand exactly.
- Have no product, no direction and no content.
- Want work outside safe platforms without clear agreement.
- Ask for free complete work before paying.
- Send suspicious links.
- Demand urgent work without fair payment.

## Safe Promise

I can help improve clarity, structure, design presentation, trust elements, mobile experience and conversion potential.

## Do Not Promise

- Guaranteed sales
- Guaranteed revenue
- Guaranteed conversion rates
- Guaranteed traffic
- Guaranteed ad results
- Guaranteed SEO ranking
