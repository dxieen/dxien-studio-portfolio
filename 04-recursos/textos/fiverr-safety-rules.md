# Fiverr Safety Rules — Dxien Studio

## Main Rule

If the order does not appear in the official Fiverr Orders section, it is not a real order.

## Do Not Do

- Do not open suspicious external links.
- Do not share email, phone number or personal information.
- Do not accept payment outside Fiverr.
- Do not start work before the order is active.
- Do not download unknown files from strangers.
- Do not log in through links sent by buyers.
- Do not scan QR codes sent by buyers.
- Do not send bank information.
- Do not continue conversations that try to move outside Fiverr.

## Suspicious Signs

- Buyer says “I placed the order” but no order appears.
- Buyer sends Google Docs before an order exists.
- Buyer asks for email or WhatsApp.
- Buyer sends a login link.
- Buyer asks to pay outside Fiverr.
- Buyer asks for urgent work without an active order.
- Buyer sends files unrelated to the Gig.
- Buyer offers unusually high payment immediately.

## Correct Action

1. Check Orders.
2. If no order exists, do not start.
3. Send a safe response.
4. Report spam if needed.
5. Block if the user keeps insisting.

## Safe Response

Hi, thanks for reaching out. For safety, I can only start once the order appears officially in my Fiverr Orders section. Please place the order directly through Fiverr, and I’ll be happy to help once it is active.
