## What to Watch

- If impressions are 0 after several days, the Gig may need better tags, category or visibility.
- If impressions exist but clicks are 0, the main image or title may need improvement.
- If clicks exist but no messages/orders, the packages, description or trust level may need improvement.
- If messages are mostly spam, report and do not click external links.

## Current Strategy

- Do not change prices during the first few days.
- Do not over-edit the Gig daily.
- Check messages 2–3 times per day.
- Respond professionally and safely.
- Track metrics once per day.
