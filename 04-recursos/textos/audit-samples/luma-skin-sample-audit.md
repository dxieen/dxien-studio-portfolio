## 4. First Impression Review

### What works

- The page looks clean and professional.
- The color palette fits the skincare/beauty niche.
- The product card gives the page a premium feeling.
- The main headline is easy to read.
- The CTA is visible and not aggressive.

### What can improve

- The product benefit could be clearer in the first screen.
- The visitor should understand faster what the product does.
- The trust elements could be more specific.
- The hero section could include a stronger benefit statement.

### Recommendation

The first screen should quickly answer:

1. What is this product?
2. Who is it for?
3. What problem does it help with?
4. What should the visitor do next?

The current hero is visually strong, but the messaging can become more product-focused.

Suggested direction:

> A simple cooling skincare tool designed to refresh your routine and help your skin feel calmer and cleaner.

## 5. Product Clarity

### Current situation

The page shows a skincare product concept clearly, but the visitor may need more context to understand the exact product function.

### Issues found

- The product visual is clean but slightly abstract.
- The page does not immediately explain how the product is used.
- The main benefit could be more specific.
- The product promise is safe, but could be more memorable.

### Recommendations

- Add a short product explanation near the hero.
- Add 3 clear benefit bullets.
- Include a simple “how it works” section.
- Use plain language instead of vague beauty claims.
- Make the product use case obvious.

### Suggested benefit bullets

- Helps refresh your skincare routine
- Easy to use at home
- Designed for a calm, clean self-care moment
- Simple product presentation for beauty shoppers

### Suggested short product description

> Luma Skin is a clean skincare landing page concept designed to present a beauty product in a simple, trustworthy and mobile-friendly way. The page focuses on clarity, product presentation and a smooth path toward action.

## 10. Mobile Experience Review

### What works

- The mobile layout is readable.
- The CTA is easy to see.
- The page keeps a clean structure.
- The design does not feel too crowded.

### Mobile issues found

- Some sections may need tighter spacing.
- Product details should appear before too much scrolling.
- CTA repetition could be improved.
- Trust messages should be near the first CTA.

### Mobile recommendations

- Keep the main CTA visible early.
- Add trust text near the CTA.
- Use short paragraphs.
- Keep benefit bullets easy to scan.
- Avoid long sections before product details.
- Add final CTA after FAQ.

### Priority mobile fix

Add a trust message directly below or near the first CTA.

Example:

> Secure checkout · Clear product details · Support available

## 11. FAQ / Objection Handling

FAQ sections help answer doubts before visitors leave.

### Questions the page should answer

- What is the product?
- How do I use it?
- Who is it for?
- What is included?
- Is checkout secure?
- How long does shipping take?
- Can I contact support?

### Recommended FAQ examples

Q: How do I use this product?  
A: Use this product as part of your regular skincare routine. Keep the instructions simple and easy to follow.

Q: What is included with my order?  
A: Clearly list what the customer receives in the package.

Q: Is checkout secure?  
A: Yes, checkout should clearly communicate secure payment and customer protection.

Q: How long does shipping take?  
A: Add a clear estimated delivery time based on the store’s shipping policy.

Q: Can I contact support if I need help?  
A: Yes, customers should know how to contact support before and after ordering.
